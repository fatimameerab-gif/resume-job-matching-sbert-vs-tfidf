Hyper-parameters: inner resume-grouped 3-fold CV on training pairs, selection by inner AUC. Threshold: max balanced accuracy on Platt-calibrated inner OOF scores. 'acc_default_threshold/recall_default_threshold' = the fitted model's own predict() (RF: 0.5 on its probability scale) to show the old artefact. Design B blocks come from repeated splits of ONE pairs file (job texts can repeat across train/test) and are not independent samples of the population: use them for ranking classifiers, not as precise population estimates. Gradient boosting = sklearn HistGradientBoosting (LightGBM/XGBoost not installed).
